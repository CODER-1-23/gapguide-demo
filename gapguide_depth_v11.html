Important safety limitation
This is an experimental student demonstration, not a mobility aid. Depth Anything V2 Small estimates relative depth from one image; it does not provide calibrated distance in metres. Near/medium/far labels are approximate scene-relative categories and can be wrong. The camera may miss stairs, holes, kerbs, glass, drop-offs, traffic and other hazards. Never use this prototype to guide real-world walking or replace a white cane, guide dog or mobility training.
Keep the phone stationary during tests. The depth model runs periodically to reduce processing load.
